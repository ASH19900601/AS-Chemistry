# Sample mark scheme for testing AI SQ Grader.
# Written for this project in the style of an AS Chemistry paper; it is not a Cambridge document.
#
# Format
#   QUESTION <question number> [<maximum mark>]
#   <point ID> [<marks>] <what the answer must contain>      alternatives are separated by /
#   RULE <point ID> DEPENDS ON <point ID>                    the first point is only awarded
#                                                            if the second is also awarded
QUESTION 1(a) [2]
P1 [1] protons and neutrons are in the nucleus
P2 [1] electrons are in shells / energy levels around the nucleus

QUESTION 1(b) [1]
P1 [1] isotopes have the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons

QUESTION 2(a) [3]
P1 [1] moles of NaOH = 0.0250 x 0.100 = 0.00250 mol
P2 [1] moles of HCl = 0.00250 mol because the reacting ratio is 1 : 1
P3 [1] concentration of HCl = 0.100 mol dm-3
RULE P3 DEPENDS ON P2

QUESTION 2(b) [2]
P1 [1] the rate of reaction increases at a higher temperature
P2 [1] more particles have energy greater than or equal to the activation energy

QUESTION 3 [2]
P1 [1] a covalent bond is a shared pair of electrons
P2 [1] electrostatic attraction between the shared pair of electrons and both nuclei
